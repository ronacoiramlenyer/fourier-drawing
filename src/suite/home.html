New here? A good path

  1. Open the Function Grapher. The first lesson, on straight lines, starts by itself. Press Next to move through it.
  2. Try the Sine waves lesson. It shows what a single wave is made of.
  3. Finish with A square wave from sines: adding waves builds new shapes.
  4. Then open Fourier Circle Studio, where each wave becomes a rotating circle and the circles draw pictures.

How the two tools connect

A point going around a circle, seen from the side, moves up and down in a sine wave. The grapher shows those waves one formula at a time. The studio chains the circles together, and with enough of them they can trace any closed outline.
